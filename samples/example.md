# Building in Public
Learn how to document your journey and grow an audience while building your next project.

---

# Why Build in Public?
It creates accountability, attracts early users, and builds community around what you're making. Plus, the feedback loop accelerates your learning.

---

# Share Your Progress
Post weekly updates about what you learned, what broke, and what you're building next. Keep it honest and vulnerable.

---

# Find Your People
Your audience isn't random. They're people solving similar problems. Keep showing up, and they'll keep coming back.

---

# Iterate Based on Feedback
Listen to your audience. Ask questions, get validation, and pivot when you learn something new. Building in public is a conversation, not a broadcast.

---

# Start Small
You don't need millions of followers to start. One engaged person who cares about your work is worth more than passive scrollers. Begin where you are.
